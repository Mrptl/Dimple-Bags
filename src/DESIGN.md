## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, bold, and modern industrial aesthetic tailored for heavy-duty B2B manufacturing, polymer synthesis, and industrial packaging logistics. The visual tone bridges technical engineering credibility with corporate packaging confidence, moving decisively away from generic, dated factory templates into an agile, ISO-compliant industrial architecture.

The system targets procurement officers, enterprise logistics directors, packaging engineers, and supply chain executives. Key emotional impressions include:
- **Absolute Material Reliability**: Sturdy layouts, calibrated structural lines, and authoritative industrial geometry.
- **Engineered Precision**: Monospaced data labels, clear specification matrices, and strict visual tolerances.
- **Operational Modernity**: Crisp contrast, deep structural navy surfaces, vivid safety accents, and tactile utility controls.

Drawing primarily from **Corporate / Modern** principles infused with **Industrial Functionalism**, the interface emphasizes structured grid cards, calibrated tabular data, and high-visibility action anchors.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from heavy-duty synthetic polymers, industrial safety indicators, and enterprise corporate logistics:

- **Primary (`#1E40AF` / `royal-blue` `#2563EB`)**: Inspired by the established brand mark, royal blue acts as the anchor for institutional authority, certification highlights, primary navigation headers, and technical validations.
- **Secondary (`#F97316` / `safety-orange` `#EA580C`)**: High-visibility safety amber/orange serves as the primary operational call-to-action color, driving RFQ submissions, instant estimate triggers, and high-urgency inventory updates.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A` / `industrial-slate` `#1E293B`)**: Ultra-deep industrial navy and slate anchor footers, hero backdrops, spec table header rows, and high-contrast typography.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` / `surface-canvas` `#F8FAFC`)**: Crisp slate-tinted light gray canvas with pure `#FFFFFF` layered card surfaces, preventing visual fatigue across dense technical data sheets and production volume tables.
- **Named Accents**: Specialized tokens provide instant visual cues for ISO 9001 compliance badges, technical batch verification tags, and industrial grade badges.

## Typography

The type system blends the technical, geometric authority of **Space Grotesk** for headlines, section tags, industrial statistics, and form controls with the supreme legibility of **Inter** for dense product specifications, packaging dimension tables, and narrative copy.

- **Headlines & Technical Metrics (`Space Grotesk`)**: Conveys architectural engineering, plant machinery confidence, and structural rigor. Used with tight negative letter tracking to provide commanding headlines on hero banners and category headers.
- **Body & Data Sheets (`Inter`)**: Guarantees optimal scanability for complex RFQ input forms, tolerance measurements (microns, GSM, tensile strength), and shipping terms across all resolutions.
- **Technical Badges & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase `label-technical` with intentional letter-spacing (+0.08em) to evoke precision factory stamps, DIN/ISO standards, and lot tracking numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-tier industrial offerings (such as LDPE Films, VCI Bags, Jumbo Liner Bags, and Pallet wraps) side-by-side with instant technical summaries.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max-width container capped at 1280px. High-density metrics and multi-field estimate calculators run seamlessly in 2-column or 3-column structural splits.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px margin. Card clusters reflow into balanced 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 16px page margins. Technical matrices enable horizontal pan or stacked summary cards.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous section spacing (`space-3xl` / 72px) separates distinct product manufacturing verticals, preserving clarity and focus during rapid quoting processes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, structured industrial tiers with low-contrast structural outlines paired with subtle, calibrated shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base`: Pure canvas background (`#F8FAFC`).
  - `Level 1 (Card & Module)`: Solid `#FFFFFF` bordered with `#E2E8F0` (1px solid), accompanied by an ambient shadow `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
  - `Level 2 (Hover & Focus Cards)`: Raised module elevation `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` with the border accent transitioning to `royal-blue` or `safety-amber`.
  - `Level 3 (Sticky Calculators, Dropdowns & Modals)`: Floating utility depth `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Industrial Contrast Striping**: In hero banners and estimate summary callouts, deep slate (`#0F172A`) surfaces are framed with razor-thin safety-orange structural highlights to command immediate priority.

## Shapes

To mirror precision manufacturing, extruded synthetic film reels, and corrugated structural packaging, the shape language commits to **Soft (Level 1)**:

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, badges, select menus) use `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radii.
- Packaging product cards, quote calculator containers, and modal dialogs use `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Full round pills (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for ISO status tags, verification chips, and active metric pills to create instant shape differentiation from operational rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (RFQ & Quote Calculator)**: Safety Orange (`#EA580C`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `Space Grotesk` semi-bold, 4px corner radius, with a subtle hover transition to `#F97316` and a crisp inner glow. Focus state: 2px offset ring in `#EA580C`.
- **Secondary Action (Spec Sheets & Direct Call)**: Industrial Slate (`#1E293B`) or Royal Blue (`#2563EB`) solid fill, white text, delivering balanced secondary weight.
- **Tertiary / Technical Outline**: White card background, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Badges & Technical Chips
- **ISO 9001 / Quality Standard Badge**: Background `#EFF6FF`, border 1px solid `#BFDBFE`, text `#1D4ED8`, uppercase with leading checkmark icon.
- **Material Metric Chips (e.g., LDPE, HM-HDPE, VCI, 50-150 Micron)**: Slate canvas `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, dark slate text `#334155`.

### Product & Vertical Cards
- Encased in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border `#E2E8F0`. Features a top-aligned category index number (e.g., `01.`, `02.`) in `label-technical` Space Grotesk, followed by bold product title, bulleted material properties, high-definition machinery/packaging photography, and an embedded spec drawer trigger.

### RFQ & Estimate Calculator Forms
- Form inputs feature 1px solid `#CBD5E1` borders, pure white surface, 4px radius, and 12px 16px internal padding.
- Focused state applies a 1px outline in `#2563EB` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` halo.
- Include integrated unit selectors (Kilograms, Metric Tons, Micron gauge, Roll width) within combined segmented controls.

### Metric Counter Displays
- Used in factory capability sections: oversized numeric value in `#0F172A` with an accent underline in `#EA580C`, paired with concise engineering descriptions in `#64748B`.
