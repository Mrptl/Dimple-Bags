About Dimple Bags & Synthetics Private Limited

Established in 2009, Dimple Bags & Synthetics Private Limited has evolved into a premier manufacturer, wholesaler, and technical supplier of high-quality plastic packaging, heavy LDPE liner bags, VCI rust prevention films, wooden pallets, and corrugated export cartons.

Under the visionary guidance of Mr. Yogesh Patel (Admin Executive), the company has steadily expanded its manufacturing infrastructure in Halol GIDC, establishing an exemplary reputation in competitive domestic and international markets.

Backed by certified testing facilities, automated co-extrusion towers, and rigorous quality control protocols, we ensure that every single product meets defined performance criteria. From standard high-yield films to custom engineered protective solutions, our goal remains steadfast: to protect your valuable products, optimize your operational costs, and accelerate your business growth.
